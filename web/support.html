Frequently Asked Questions

How do I keep audio playing with the screen locked?
Audio continues playing automatically when you lock your device. Ensure Silent mode or Do Not Disturb settings allow media playback, and you can play, pause, or skip directly from your lock screen.
How do I set the Sleep Timer?
While listening to any story in the player screen, tap the crescent moon timer icon in the top toolbar to choose 15, 30, 45, 60 minutes, or "End of Story". The story will gently fade out when the timer ends.
Can I request a story or theme for Barbara to record?
Yes! Barbara loves hearing bedtime suggestions from listeners. Send your favorite folklore, classic childhood tale, or tranquil theme to colinrafter82@gmail.com.
How do I restore purchases or manage my subscription?
All purchases and subscriptions are handled securely through Apple's App Store. You can manage or cancel subscriptions anytime in your iPhone / iPad Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.
Is any personal data collected while I listen?
No. We do not sell data, track your location, or use third-party advertising trackers. Your listening habits remain completely private.
