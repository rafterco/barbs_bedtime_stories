Privacy Policy

Barbara's Sleep Stories • Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Barbara's Sleep Stories ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a serene, stress-free sleep sanctuary. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

We believe your sleep and relaxation habits are private. We do not collect, sell, or rent your personal identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical location, or phone number) to third parties.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a seamless bedtime experience, the app stores certain preferences locally on your device:

  • Favorites: Stories and playlists you bookmark for quick bedtime access.
  • Downloaded Audio: Offline audio files stored locally so you can listen without an internet connection.
  • App Preferences: Your default sleep timer selections, ambient mixer volume levels, and auto-play choices.

4. Cloud Streaming and Media Playback

Our audio narrations, cover artwork, and soundscapes are hosted via secure cloud storage (Firebase / Google Cloud). When you stream a story, standard secure network requests (HTTPS) are made to stream the audio file to your device. We do not track your identity during streaming.

5. Device Permissions

Our app only utilizes standard permissions strictly necessary for audio functionality:

  • Foreground Service / Audio Playback: Allows stories and calming ambient sounds to continue playing smoothly while your screen is off or while using other apps.
  • Internet Access: Used exclusively to stream story audio and fetch library updates.
  • Notifications: Used on Android 13+ solely to show lock-screen and notification shade media playback controls (play, pause, rewind).

6. Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase services (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage) for hosting app content and catalog data. These providers process data in compliance with strict industry-standard security and privacy practices.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app provides relaxing audio stories designed for peaceful relaxation and sleep. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
