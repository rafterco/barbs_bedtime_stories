About the App

A quiet bedtime listening app made for peaceful evenings and restful sleep.

🌙

Simple, Peaceful Bedtime Listening

Barbara's Sleep Stories was created to give listeners a calm, comforting voice at the end of the day. No loud intros, no popups, and no ads interrupting your rest—just quiet bedtime stories and gentle background sounds to help you relax, close your eyes, and fall asleep.

🎙️ Real Voice Read by Barbara with a warm, steady, and unhurried pace.
🌧️ Background Sounds Add quiet rain, ocean waves, or a fireplace while you listen.
🌙 Screen-Off Audio Lock your phone and listen in the dark without screen glare.

The voice behind the stories

A bit about Barb

"A quiet place for your busy mind to go before sleep."

Barb has spent much of her career helping people find their way toward greater well-being, drawing on her background in Psychology and Therapeutic Recreation. As a rehabilitation therapist, Barb has built her practice on goal driven activation and art-based expressive therapies to encourage emotional regulation, cognitive rehab, and connection. A large part of her work with individuals living with brain injury has also included coaching clients in sleep hygiene and healthy sleep routines, as well as using art-based expressive therapies to encourage creativity, connection, and self-expression.

Barb has seen how deeply sleep can affect our mood, energy, thinking, and ability to enjoy everyday life. The development of “Sleep Stories” stems from a genuine desire to help people discover simple, practical ways to sleep better—and feel better, too.

A quiet place for your busy mind to go before sleep.

Good sleep is essential to our well-being, helping support memory, learning, mood, concentration, and our ability to manage the demands of everyday life. Yet for many of us, slowing down enough to fall asleep can be surprisingly difficult. A calming bedtime routine and relaxing activities can help create the transition from wakefulness to rest. This app offers adults a gentle way to step away from the busyness of the day through engaging, soothing stories written especially for bedtime. Settle in, let the story take you somewhere else, and give your busy mind a quiet place to go before sleep.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Who is Barbara? ▼
Barbara is our bedtime storyteller. Having read bedtime stories to her children and grandchildren for over thirty years, she brings that same calm, comforting presence to every story in the app.
Will audio keep playing when my phone screen is locked? ▼
Yes. Both iOS and Android versions support background playback and lock-screen controls so you can keep your bedroom completely dark.
Can I play ambient sounds alongside stories? ▼
Yes. You can independently adjust the volume of gentle rain, crackling fireplace, ocean waves, and heartbeat sounds while stories play.
Are there ads in the app? ▼
No. There are no advertisements, intrusive popups, or marketing trackers to disrupt your sleep.
